Heads Up For Tails

Reframing pet parenting

Category Brand Strategy Year 2026

The challenge

Pet parenting is becoming more intentional, but also more overwhelming. With endless advice on food, health, grooming and care, pet parents today don't just need another brand selling products. They need confidence in the choices they make.

The approach

I identified the emotional and functional tensions of modern pet parenting and found a role for HUFT beyond retail: a trusted partner that helps pet parents navigate the journey.

The concept

Yours, Truly
There's no perfect way to be a pet parent. But we'll figure it out together.

A campaign positioning HUFT as the co-parenting partner that helps pet parents navigate the questions, uncertainties and everyday moments that come with caring for a pet.

The concept comes to life through education, expert-backed advice, relatable storytelling and community.

Mile Collective

Reimagining the online activewear experience

Category Brand Strategy Year 2026

The challenge

Activewear is no longer just for the gym. But shopping for it online still comes with a question: will it actually work for me?

Mile has built its proposition around comfort, functionality and the realities of Indian women. The opportunity was to translate that product truth into a more confidence-building digital experience.

The approach

Identify the gap between liking a product and imagining yourself wearing it.

I looked at consumer conversations, Mile's product proposition and the online activewear journey to explore how the brand could make fit, function and versatility easier to see, understand and trust.

The concept

See Yourself in Mile
A brand experience platform designed to turn "I like it" into "I can see myself in it" through three layers:

See It: Real women, real proportions, real ways to wear it.
Understand It: Make Mile's fit, fabric, construction and functionality easier to experience.
Try It: Use AI-powered visualisation to help shoppers imagine the product on themselves before buying.

This comes to life through UGC, product storytelling, social content and an AI virtual try-on pilot.

MU Cosmetics

Exploring a new direction for MU Cosmetics

Category Brand Strategy Year 2024
Add cover image

The challenge

MU Cosmetics already had the ingredients, formulations and science to back its products. MU Cosmetics was struggling to drive sales and build resonance with younger consumers.

The brand faced a larger question: was the problem with the products, the communication, or the positioning itself? We explored whether MU needed a complete brand revamp or a sharper evolution of what already existed.

The approach

Start with the consumer before changing the brand.

We combined qualitative and quantitative research — interviewing 5 women and surveying 21 consumers to understand perceptions of MU's packaging, messaging, products, values and overall brand appeal.

These insights helped identify where the brand was losing relevance and where there was an opportunity to reconnect with younger consumers.

The concept

Rather than starting from scratch, we explored how MU could better communicate the science, ingredients and product value it already had to a younger consumer.

The strategy explored target audiences & personas, product and packaging, social, PR, budget allocation and offline retail.

FabIndia

Evaluating Fabindia Through an ESG & Consumer Lens

Category Case Study Year 2025

The concept

An in-depth analysis of Fabindia's existing STP and sustainability positioning, examining its ESG initiatives and alignment with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The study further explored the societal classification of its products through the SHIFT framework, alongside primary research with Fabindia consumers to uncover perceptions, motivations and barriers. It identified key consumer and brand-side dilemmas and translated these findings into strategic recommendations for strengthening Fabindia's sustainability proposition.

Sid's Farm

Let Milk Just Be Milk

Category Print Year 2025

The concept

With dairy adulteration becoming an increasing consumer concern, the campaign positions Sid's Farm as a brand that doesn't shy away from the conversation. "Let Milk Just Be Milk" uses OOH to turn transparency into the core creative idea. The idea is to address adulteration through facts, educate consumers on what to look for, and show how Sid's Farm ensures the purity of its milk. The design is deliberately straightforward and no-nonsense, with a consumer-first philosophy.

Brochure: Environmental Psychology Course

India's Biggest Challenge: Purifying the Purest River

Category Print Year 2025

The concept

A research-backed awareness brochure exploring the environmental degradation of the Ganga and the human behaviours contributing to it. Drawing insights from three research papers, the brochure translates complex findings into accessible information on pollution, its ecological impact, and the consequences for communities that depend on the river. It concludes with realistic, actionable recommendations, bridging research with practical solutions to encourage greater environmental responsibility.
